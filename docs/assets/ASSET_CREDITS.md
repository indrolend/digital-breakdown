separately so absence of a credit is never mistaken for project ownership.

## Project-created temporary assets

The neutral audio cues, music/ambience loops, abstract TV signals, and generic
phone model are generated by `tools/generate-release-safe-placeholders.mjs`.
They are project-owned under AGPL-3.0-only, contain no sampled source media, and
are marked `TEMPORARY` because their artistic direction is provisional.

## Attribution placement

For official releases, include these credits:

- in a bundled `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES` file;
- in an accessible in-game credits view when feasible;
- in source-distribution documentation;
- in storefront or website notices where the license reasonably requires it.

Credits supplement the corresponding license text; they do not replace it.
